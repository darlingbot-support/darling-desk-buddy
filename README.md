# Darling's Digital Heart

"A little companion for your everyday space." with subtitle, "Join early access" primary CTA, and an interactive robot visual framed in a luminous halo.
- Why Darling: Emotional narrative on the functional desk versus gentle desk presence, offline-first and app-free.
- Interactive Mood Demo: Functional interactive face simulator letting visitors trigger moods (Curious, Focus, Sleepy, Surprised, Happy) with animated expressive eyes, blinks, and dynamic descriptions.
- Feature Story: Editorial cards for Expressive Face, Mood System, Touch & Motion reactivity, Clock Mode, Focus Mode, Mini-Games, Local Wake Word, and 100% Offline privacy.
- Early Access Section: Email signup form with instant confirmation ("You’re on Darling’s early list. We’ll keep you close to the first drop."), transparent target offer (₹3,999), and planned launch date (23 October 2026).
- Footer: Social channels (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X) and support email (darlingbot.support@gmail.com).
